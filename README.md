# DreamQuest (DQ)

**DragonFable, AdventureQuest and MechQuest on Android.** One app, three classic AEgames, made comfortable on a phone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Download size:** `DreamQuest.apk`, about 66 MB.
>
> | | Minimum | Recommended |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | **Android** | 9.0 | 12 or newer |
> | **Memory (RAM)** | 4 GB | 8 GB |
> | **Free storage** | 300 MB | 500 MB |
> | **Other** | Up-to-date Android System WebView, internet connection | Stable Wi-Fi or mobile data |

## Download and install

1. Download `DreamQuest.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open the file on your phone and allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks.
3. Launch **DreamQuest**, pick a game and log in with your usual game account.

## What you get

- **All three games** in one app, loaded straight from the official servers.
- **Phone-friendly controls:** side bars, a Touch UI for DragonFable, on-screen keyboard handling and controller support.
- **Graphics options:** resolution, frame rate, upscaling, color and lighting effects, with a Vanilla switch to turn everything off.
- **Helpers:** a Today panel for your dailies, Monster Info and an in-app Guide.
- **Stays alive in the background**, so switching apps does not log you out.
